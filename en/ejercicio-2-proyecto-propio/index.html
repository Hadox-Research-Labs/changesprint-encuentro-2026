The complete sequence

We do not repeat the lesson. We remove the scaffolding.

  1. LessonLearn

    Chat, Work, Codex, folders, instructions, skills, tools, permissions and tests.

  2. Exercise 1Operate

    Follow a prepared problem with rules, fictional data, formats and five predefined cases.

  3. Exercise 2Design

    Use your own canvas to choose the task, justify the boundaries and create the tests.

The assignment

Configure a small agent addressing one evidenced task in your project.

Do not begin by creating a skill.

First demonstrate that you understand the process. The agent follows from that understanding; it does not replace it.

Workbook

Six pieces of evidence. One defensible proposal.

Each step produces a reviewable file. A demonstration existing only in the conversation with the AI is not accepted.

Suggested follow-up session · 120 minutes

Spend the time making decisions, not touring buttons.

  1. 00–20Audit the canvas and classify facts, hypotheses and questions.
  2. 20–40Map the process and locate the human decision.
  3. 40–60Choose the task and complete the agent contract.
  4. 60–90Configure the Codex prototype using fictional data.
  5. 90–110Design tests, provoke a failure and correct it.
  6. 110–120Present changed judgement, evidence and residual risk.

Passing criterion

The most impressive agent does not win.

The winning team can demonstrate what it knows, what it still assumes, what it delegated, what it prohibited, how it tested and who retains the decision.
